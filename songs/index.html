Song Writing

Since learning to play bass guitar as part of the worship team at my church, I've also discovered a passion for writing worship songs with a focus on lyrics inspired by biblical passages. The lyrics are 100% my personal creative work product, and I lean on AI music tools to add vocals and instrumental tracks to my song projects. I love to see vivid stories come to life in a song, and important truths about faith put to melody. When a song gets stuck in your head ... that message will have a chance to anchor itself in your heart as well.

Another avenue that I began exploring for song writing is as a contributor to the Songs of Love Charity. This charity works with volunteer song writers like myself to write custom songs for children and elderly individuals going through difficult medical journeys.

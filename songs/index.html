Song Writing

Since retiring from my corporate career, I've learned to play the cajon and bass guitar, and frequently have the opportunity to serve on the worship team at my church. I've also discovered a passion for writing worship songs with a focus on lyrics inspired by biblical passages. Some of the songs I’ve written have even been performed by friends and colleagues at their churches.

I love to see vivid stories come together in a song, and important truths about faith put to melody. When a song gets stuck in your head, that message will have a chance to anchor itself in your heart as well. Music is one of the most effective ways to share God’s word and let his message be heard, understood and spread freely. Our Worship Wagon ministry has shown me just how impactful music can be on a weary soul.

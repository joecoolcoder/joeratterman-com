By Faith

Hebrews 11

Intro

By faith, by our faith … Your will be done

Verse 1

By faith, by his faith … Noah sailed the flood
And Your people - they walked the earth again
By faith, by his faith … Moses led them out
And Your people - they walked the promised land

Verse 2

By faith, by his faith … Daniel braved the den
You shut the lion’s mouth - and he walked out
By faith, by his faith … Abraham obeyed
His son, an offering - walked home that day

Chorus

By our faith … we trust in Your plan
When we walk by faith, Your will be done
By our faith … we trust in Your plan
When we walk by faith, Your will be done

Verse 3

By faith, by her faith … Mary raised Your son
Jesus became a man - and walked with us
By faith, by his faith … He endured the cross
We were redeemed – when He walked … out of that grave!

Bridge

When we wait for the promise, we walk by faith
When we hope for the future, we walk by faith
When prayers aren’t yet answered, we walk by faith
Your will be done, when we walk by faith

Chorus

By our faith … we trust in Your plan
When we walk by faith, Your will be done
By our faith … we trust in Your plan
When we walk by faith, Your will be done

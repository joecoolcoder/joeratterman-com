Give Them Away

1 Peter 4:10

Verse

If I teach, let me speak Your truth.
If I serve, let me serve like You.
If I lead, let me lead in love.
All I am, Lord, is from above.

Chorus

You gave us gifts, each in our own way,
Lord help us give them away.
We give for your glory, we give to obey.
Each heart a gift, through grace each day,
Lord help us give them away.

Verse

When I give, show me those in need.
When I’m wise, let my wisdom spread.
When I’m merciful, bring me those in pain.
All I do Lord, is for your name.

Chorus

You gave us gifts, each in our own way,
Lord help us give them away.
We give for your glory, we give to obey.
Each heart a gift, through grace each day,
Lord help us give them away.

Bridge

We give for your glory, not for our own.
We give our gifts, Lord, to make You known.
We need You, Father, we need one another,
We give your gifts, Lord, to serve our brother.

Verse

If I heal, bring me your wounded.
If I encourage, help me cheer them on.
If I sing, give me songs to declare.
All I have, Lord, is Yours to share.

Chorus

Lord help us give them away.

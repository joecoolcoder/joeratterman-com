Sing to the Lord

Psalm 95

Chorus

Come! - Let us sing to the Lord,
Our rock, our rock of salvation.
Shout! - Shout with Joy to the Lord,
Our rock, our rock of salvation.
We shout, we sing, with all creation,
To our rock, our rock of salvation.

Verse 1

We come to Your throne, Oh King of kings,
Grateful for, the song in our souls.
You hold creation, in Your mighty hands.
From ocean depths, to mountain peaks,
May all the earth, make a joyful noise.

Verse 2

Come bow down, in humble worship,
Harden not your hearts, when you hear his voice.
All praise and glory, to the lord above,
Who was before, and is to come.
We are his people, and He is our God.

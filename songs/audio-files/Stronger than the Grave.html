Stronger than the Grave

Song of Solomon

Verse 1

In the darkness and loneliness of the night,
Yearning … I search for Your light.
Waiting in silence, You seem so far away,
I need you Lord - bring me near today.

Verse 2

In my longing, Your love held true.
Your voice kept calling me, drawing me back to you!
My heart leaps, the moment arrives!
Restored with my beloved ... My hope revived!

Chorus

You conquered death for my soul, love stronger than the grave.
Oceans and rivers can’t wash it away.
Your love my King, can’t be bought, can never be earned.
A seal on your heart with a love that burns.
You are mine, I am Yours - love stronger than the grave.

Verse 3

You call me chosen, by grace You call me Yours,
No more searching, now Your mercy flows.
Apart, too long my Lord, You drew me in,
Your love kindles the fire within.

Chorus

You conquered death for my soul, love stronger than the grave.
Oceans and rivers can’t wash it away.
Your love my King, can’t be bought, can never be earned.
A seal on your heart with a love that burns.
You are mine, I am Yours - love stronger than the grave.

Outro

Your love, like flashes of fire, the very flame of my Lord,
You are mine, I am Yours, love stronger than the grave.

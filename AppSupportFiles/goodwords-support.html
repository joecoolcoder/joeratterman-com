A Little Wisdom, Inspiration, and Humor for Your Day!

Features

  • A daily Bible passage, quote, clean joke, and Today in History events
  • Additional passages and quotes when available
  • Favorite verses you can add, import, and revisit
  • Passage lookup and comparison across nine Bible translations
  • Passage study links and full-chapter reading choices
  • Brief Reflections, Passage Commentaries, and Archives
  • A Home Screen widget for today’s verse and an available favorite verse
  • Sharing and favorite-verse export

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I see more daily readings?

Good Words shows additional passages and quotes when they are available. On the Home screen, tap the page circles or swipe left and right to move through a section’s readings.

What if I am offline?

When today’s download is unavailable, Good Words can show a reading from its small built-in offline collection. Internet feeds, passage lookups, and online study links need a connection.

How do Favorites work?

Open Favorites from the Home screen menu to save a verse or add your own. Favorite verses are stored on your device. Choose how often they appear in Settings, or pin one to keep it on the Home screen.

How do I look up a passage?

Open Favorites, choose Add Verse, and enter a reference to preview or compare translations. A verse’s Tools menu on the Home screen offers study links, full-chapter reading, and passage insights.

What does the widget show?

The widget shows today’s verse. When a favorite or pinned verse appears on the Home screen, the widget also offers a Fav choice. Tap Daily or Fav to switch between them.

Where are my favorite verses stored?

Good Words stores them on your device and does not sync them through a Good Words account. You can export favorite-verse references from the app. Your device’s own backup settings may also include app data.

Privacy

Good Words does not require an account with the developer and does not include advertising or in-app tracking. It uses online services for daily content and passage tools; favorite verses stay on your device.

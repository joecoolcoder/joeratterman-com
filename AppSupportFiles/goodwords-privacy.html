Good Words Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Good Words mobile apps for iOS and Android (the “Apps”) handle information when you use daily readings, favorite verses, online tools, and widgets.

1. Accounts, Advertising, and Tracking

Good Words, provided by Joe Cool Coder, does not require an account. The Apps do not include advertising, tracking, or an in-app analytics SDK. If you email support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your message.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The Apps store your favorite verses, including saved translation text and pin choices; settings; and downloaded readings and lookup caches on your device. A widget can read the verse and translation preferences made available to it through storage shared with the app.

Your complete Favorites library is not sent to GoodWords.Today for daily-content selection. You can edit or delete favorite verses in the app. A particular verse reference or passage may be sent when you choose an online lookup or commentary, as described below.

3. Daily Content and Internet Feeds

The Apps and widgets request daily readings from GoodWords.Today. That service also supplies any available bonus verses and quotes from outside providers. These requests do not upload your Favorites or app settings. GoodWords.Today and its hosting provider receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and the requested URL, to deliver and protect the service.

If an online service is unavailable, some readings may not appear. The Apps can use a small built-in collection when the primary daily download is unavailable.

4. Bible Passage Lookups and External Links

When you preview, compare, or display a Bible passage that needs a lookup, an App may send its reference and requested translation to a Bible text provider, including Crossway, YouVersion, Tyndale, or API.Bible. The request does not include your complete Favorites library. The provider receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, and handles it under its own practices.

If you request a Brief Reflection or Passage Commentary that is not already available, the App sends the reference and displayed passage text to GoodWords.Today. The service sends that text to OpenAI to generate the requested content. It does not send your complete Favorites library.

If you follow a study, full-chapter, copyright, archive, or other external link, the destination may receive information in the link and ordinary connection information. An author search also includes the author's name in the search URL. Those destinations have their own privacy practices.

5. Retention, Deletion, and Device Backups

Good Words does not operate an account or a separate cloud-sync service for Favorites. Your favorite verses remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app. Downloaded readings and lookup caches are replaced or pruned as the app operates. GoodWords.Today does not keep a server-side copy of your Favorites library.

Your device's operating system may include app data in a device backup, depending on your backup settings. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete an existing device backup; manage those backups through your device or platform account. Online and hosting providers may retain their own technical request logs under their policies. Contact us to ask about deletion of information we control.

6. Sharing and Your Choices

Sharing a reading or exporting favorite references uses your device's sharing controls. The recipient or service you select receives the content you choose to share. You can manage daily categories in Settings and edit or delete favorite verses in Favorites.

7. Security and Policy Changes

Good Words uses HTTPS for its internet requests and stores local files in app storage provided by your device. No internet service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date.

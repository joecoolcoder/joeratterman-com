Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Choose letters and numbers to reveal every matching character in the answer. Solve the answer before Gavin fades completely or time runs out.

Does Word Difficulty affect every word pack?

Difficulty filters word packs that include difficulty ratings. Other packs keep your preference but use their full word list.

How does Reveal work?

Reveal uncovers one undiscovered character. In single-player games, it becomes available through winning streaks. Two-player matches can enable Reveal during match setup.

Where do hints come from?

Some answers include a hint stored in the app. Single-player games may also request a definition from Free Dictionary. Two-player answers and hints are never sent for an online lookup.

Can LetterGeist be played offline?

Yes. The game and stored hints work offline. Free Dictionary definitions require an internet connection and may not be available for every answer.

How do I reset statistics?

Open Statistics and use the Reset Statistics button for all-time, current-session, or selected word-pack records.

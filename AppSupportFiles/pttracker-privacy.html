PT Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains how PT Tracker ("the App") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

PT Tracker does not require an account and does not collect personal information for the developer.

The App does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

PT Tracker stores your routines, exercise details, active progress, routine history, calendar history, and app settings locally on your iPhone so they are available the next time you use the App.

This information is not transmitted to the developer or synchronized to a developer-operated server. You can reset a routine’s progress or delete a routine within the App. Removing the App removes its locally stored information from your device.

3. Exported Routine Files

If you choose to export a routine, the App creates a file containing that routine’s configuration and exercises. You choose where to save or share that file using iOS. Exported files do not include active progress or calendar history.

Once you save or share an exported file, its handling is governed by the destination you choose, such as the Files app, a cloud storage provider, or another sharing service.

4. Network Access

PT Tracker does not require an online service to create, run, or track routines. The App does not send your activity, settings, or history to the developer. If you choose to open a web link, such as this Privacy Policy, your browser or device may use an internet connection.

5. Data Security

PT Tracker keeps its routine and progress information on your device. You are responsible for protecting your device and any routine files you choose to export or share.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

7. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to ratterman.apps@gmail.com.

Play Setlist Support

Play setlists from your own audio folders, then move songs into practice mode for key, tempo, speed, and transposition tools.

Features

  • Play audio files directly from folders selected in the Files app
  • Build a setlist automatically from every supported song in a folder
  • Use optional shuffle playback, repeat playback, and one-tap reshuffle
  • View the play order, jump to any song, and see the current track position
  • Sort setlists using numbered file names when shuffle is off
  • See song artwork, title, artist, folder name, and track count when available
  • Automatically estimate key and tempo for the current setlist song
  • Send the current setlist song into Practice mode for focused rehearsal
  • Choose a separate practice song from the Files app when needed
  • Detect song key and tempo in Practice mode
  • Transpose from one key to another and see the semitone shift
  • Adjust practice playback speed from 50% to 150%
  • Loop a practice song with repeat mode and use a speed-aware timeline
  • Background audio support with lock screen, Control Center, and Apple CarPlay controls

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Play Setlist do?

Play Setlist combines folder-based setlist playback with practice tools for musicians, singers, worship teams, and anyone preparing songs from their own audio files.

What audio formats are supported?

Play Setlist works with common audio file formats supported by iOS, including MP3, WAV, AAC, AIF, AIFF, CAF, and M4A files.

Can playback continue when the screen is locked?

Yes. Play Setlist supports background audio playback, lock screen controls, and Apple CarPlay playback controls.

How does setlist sorting work?

When shuffle is off, songs with numbered file names sort in number order. Songs without numbers appear after numbered songs.

Can I change the play order?

You can open Play Order to view upcoming songs, jump to a specific track, or reshuffle the setlist when shuffle mode is enabled.

How do Setlist and Practice work together?

When a setlist song is selected, you can switch to Practice mode and use that song for key detection, tempo detection, transposition, speed adjustment, and repeat playback.

What do key and tempo detection do?

Play Setlist estimates the musical key and tempo of selected songs locally on your device. Results are intended as helpful practice aids and may vary with recording quality or song arrangement.

Can I slow down a song without changing key?

Yes. Practice mode lets you adjust playback speed from 50% to 150% while keeping key controls separate.

Does the App send my songs anywhere?

No. Your audio files remain on your device. Playback, artwork display, key detection, and tempo detection happen locally.

Rehearse Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains how Rehearse Pro ("the App") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

Rehearse Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data to the developer or any third parties.

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party SDKs that collect data.

2. Access to Your Audio Files

Rehearse Pro allows you to choose folders and audio files using the system file picker.

The App accesses only the folders and audio files you select.

Rehearse Pro does not access any other files or folders on your device.

Audio files and artwork remain on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

3. Audio Analysis

Rehearse Pro can analyze selected audio files to estimate musical key and tempo.

Audio analysis happens locally on your device. Audio files and analysis results are not sent to the developer or any third parties.

4. Data Security

Since Rehearse Pro does not collect or transmit any data, there is no data stored on our servers.

Playback, file access, artwork display, and audio processing happen locally on your device.

5. Your Choices

You control the folders and audio files you choose in the App. You can clear selected songs or folders in the App, choose different files, or revoke file access through iOS system controls.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

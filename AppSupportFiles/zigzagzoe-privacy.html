Zig Zag Zō Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains how Zig Zag Zō ("the App") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

Zig Zag Zō does not require an account and does not collect personal information for the developer.

The App does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Zig Zag Zō stores player names, game settings, and Single Player statistics locally on your device so they can be available the next time you play.

This information is not transmitted to the developer or synchronized to a developer-operated server. You can reset statistics within the App. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored information.

3. Network Access

Zig Zag Zō is designed to be played entirely offline. The App does not send your game activity, player names, settings, or statistics to the developer and does not require an online service for gameplay.

4. Data Security

Zig Zag Zō keeps its player names, settings, and statistics on your device using storage provided by iOS. Because the App does not operate an account system or developer server, this information is not stored by the developer.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

6. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to ratterman.apps@gmail.com.

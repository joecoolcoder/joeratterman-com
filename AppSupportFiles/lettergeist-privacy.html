LetterGeist Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains how LetterGeist ("the App") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

LetterGeist does not require an account and does not collect personal information for the developer.

The App does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

LetterGeist stores game settings, statistics, word-pack progress, and player names locally on your device so they can be available the next time you play.

This information is not transmitted to the developer or synchronized to a developer-operated server. You can reset statistics within the App. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored information.

3. Two-Player Content

Secret answers and hints entered during a two-player match are used only for that match. They remain on your device and are not sent to the Free Dictionary service or any other online service.

4. Dictionary Requests

Some single-player answers include a hint stored within the App. When a dictionary definition is requested, LetterGeist sends the selected game answer to the Free Dictionary API using an encrypted HTTPS connection.

LetterGeist sends only the bundled game answer needed for the lookup and does not attach your name, game statistics, settings, or other App data. Like any internet service, the dictionary provider may receive standard network information, such as your IP address, when processing the request. The developer does not control the provider's server logs or data-handling practices.

5. AI-Generated Hint Content

Some word packs contain game hints that were generated with the assistance of artificial intelligence before the App was published. These hints are bundled with LetterGeist. The App does not send your information to an artificial intelligence service.

6. Data Security

LetterGeist keeps its settings, statistics, and game progress on your device. The App uses HTTPS when requesting an optional online dictionary definition.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to ratterman.apps@gmail.com.

Leg Logger Support

Log flight legs and keep clear records for the details pilots track on each flight.

Features

  • Record the details for each leg of a flight
  • Automatic live scanning for HOBBS, fuel, and airport IDs
  • Save multiple aircraft tail numbers on your device
  • Save up to 25 multi-leg flight profiles on your device
  • Reverse Course feature for quickly building return legs
  • Share flight details by text, email, and other system sharing options
  • Designed for quick entry while flight details are fresh
  • No account creation required

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Leg Logger do?

Leg Logger helps pilots record the details for each leg of a flight, including aircraft, route, HOBBS, fuel, and airport information.

Who is Leg Logger for?

Leg Logger is designed for pilots who want a straightforward way to track multi-leg flights and share flight details when needed.

Does Leg Logger collect personal information?

No. Leg Logger does not require account creation and does not collect personal information.

Leg Logger Support

A focused, offline way to capture flight legs, aircraft details, and optional flight information on iPhone.

Features

  • Record an active flight with tail number, starting airport, Hobbs, and fuel values
  • Add up to six flight legs with airport, Hobbs, and fuel details
  • Optionally log start fuel, start and end times, SIC, night operations, and approaches
  • Use the camera to scan airport identifiers, Hobbs readings, and supported fuel values
  • Attach, name, view, delete, and share flight images
  • Archive, edit, restore, delete, and share completed flights
  • Configure home airport, saved-flight limit, and aircraft-specific avionics and Hobbs types
  • Keep flight records and settings on your iPhone without an account or internet service

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I begin a flight?

On the Active Flight page, enter a tail number and starting Hobbs value, then add or complete leg details as needed. Your configured home airport is used as the starting airport when available.

Can I enter details manually?

Yes. All flight values can be typed directly. Camera scanning is optional and is intended to speed up certain airport, Hobbs, and fuel entries.

Why is a fuel scan not always available?

Fuel scanning depends on the avionics type configured for the current aircraft. When scanning is not supported, enter the fuel value manually.

How do I save a completed flight?

Tap Save on the Active Flight page. Saved flights appear in Archives, where you can review, edit, share, restore, or delete them.

What does Restore do?

Restore replaces the Active Flight with the archived flight you selected. The confirmation lets you save the current Active Flight first when appropriate.

How do flight images work?

Use Images to capture or manage images for a flight. You can select images to share, or export a complete flight package that includes its report and images.

Where is my data stored?

Leg Logger stores flight records, settings, and saved flight images locally on your iPhone. It does not use an account or a developer-operated online service.

Can I remove my information?

Yes. You can clear the active flight, delete archived flights, or delete individual images in the App. Removing the App also removes its locally stored information.

Privacy

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Build tableau columns downward in alternating colors. Move Aces through Kings to their matching foundations. Only Kings may be placed in empty tableau columns.

How do I switch games?

Use the game-switching control beside the current game title. Your Vegas and Classic 745 games remain separate, allowing you to move between games without losing their progress.

How does automatic movement work?

Tap an exposed card to move it to a useful legal destination. You can also drag cards and valid card sequences when you want to choose the destination yourself.

What is Auto Finish?

When every remaining card can move safely to the foundations, the Auto Finish button becomes available. You decide whether to use it; the game never finishes automatically.

Can Aces to Kings be played offline?

Yes. Aces to Kings does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

How do I reset statistics?

Open Statistics from the game's tool tray and choose the available reset option for the records you want to clear.

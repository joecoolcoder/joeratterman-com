Aces to Kings Support

A focused, elegant take on traditional solitaire for iPhone.

Features

  • Traditional Klondike solitaire gameplay
  • Vegas, Classic 745, and Relaxed game styles
  • Smart single-tap card and stack movement
  • Choice of Draw 1 or Draw 3, with pass limits
  • Replay a deal whenever you want a fresh try
  • Optional game timer and Auto Finish
  • Separate statistics for every game style
  • Configurable card backs, table appearances, sound, and haptics

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Build tableau columns downward in alternating colors. Move Aces through Kings to their matching foundations. Only Kings may be placed in empty tableau columns.

How do I choose a game style?

Tap the game title to open Settings, then choose Vegas, Classic 745, or Relaxed. You can also adjust the draw count, pass limit, and other options there.

How does automatic movement work?

Tap an exposed card to move it to a useful legal destination. You can also drag cards and valid card sequences when you want to choose the destination yourself.

What is Auto Finish?

When every remaining card can move safely to the foundations, the Auto Finish button becomes available. You decide whether to use it; the game never finishes automatically.

Can I replay a deal?

Yes. After making a move, choose Replay Game from the tool tray to return to the beginning of the current deal and try again.

What is Timed Mode?

Choose a timer in Settings to add a countdown to each deal. The progress bar can be tapped to pause or resume, and long-pressed to reset.

Can Aces to Kings be played offline?

Yes. Aces to Kings does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

How do I reset statistics?

Open Statistics from the game's tool tray and choose the available reset option for the records you want to clear.

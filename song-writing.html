Song Writing

Since retiring, I've learned to play bass guitar and frequently serve on the worship team at my church. I've also discovered a passion for writing worship songs, several of which have been adopted and performed by friends at their churches.

This page is a place to collect a few favorite songs and make them easier to hear and share. You can also hear more of my songs on Suno.

Song links open MP3 files directly, so most browsers will play them in the browser. If a browser cannot play the file, it can download the MP3 instead.
